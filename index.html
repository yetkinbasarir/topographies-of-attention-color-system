Topographies of Attention — Day / Night

The monthly color structure remains fixed across day and night. No colors are added, removed, lightened, or darkened. Night mode is built only by reassigning roles inside the same monthly set.

Day Reference

cold set

Paper, Ink, Quiet Ink

A cooler editorial neutral set for day reading on screen. Paper avoids pure white, Ink avoids absolute black, and Quiet Ink stays related to both.

Night Reference

warm set

Paper, Ink, Quiet Ink

A warmer night counterpart with lower saturation. Ink becomes the field, Paper becomes the light reading tone, and Quiet Ink stays quiet enough not to compete with the seasonal colors.
